PostGrid

Privacy policy

Updated 6 October 2026. PostGrid is a privately operated video planning and publishing application. Access is provided by its administrator.

Information used

PostGrid processes your login email, connected platform account identifiers and display names, authorization tokens, uploaded videos, descriptions, schedules, publishing preferences and job results. This information is used to authenticate you, display your connected accounts and perform publishing actions you request. Social network passwords are entered only on the platform's own authorization pages.

Storage and sharing

GitHub Pages hosts the website, Vercel runs the backend, and Supabase provides authentication, database and video storage. Social authorization tokens are encrypted on the server. Selected videos and associated metadata are transmitted to the platforms you select when publishing is enabled. Hosting providers may process technical request logs. PostGrid does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Control and deletion

You can disconnect a platform in Accounts and revoke its access in that platform's settings. Disconnecting removes the stored authorization tokens and cancels pending work for that connection; it does not remove posts already published on a social network. Other uploaded content and queue records remain until removed by the administrator. There is currently no automatic retention deadline or self-service full account deletion.

For access or deletion requests, contact the administrator who provided your PostGrid login. The project maintainer can also be reached through the PostGrid repository. Do not include passwords, tokens or private videos in public discussions.

External platforms

Connected platforms process data under their own privacy policies. PostGrid uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and Google account access controls. This policy describes the current private deployment; changes will be published on this page.
